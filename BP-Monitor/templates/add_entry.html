🩺 Blood Pressure Measurement Tips

  • Rest first: Sit quietly for 5 minutes before measuring
  • Proper position: Sit with back supported, feet flat on floor
  • Arm position: Rest arm at heart level on a table
  • Cuff placement: Place cuff on bare skin, 1 inch above elbow
  • Multiple readings: Take 2-3 readings 1 minute apart
  • Avoid: Caffeine, smoking, or exercise 30 minutes before
  • Same arm: Use the same arm each time for consistency
  • Regular timing: Measure at the same time of day

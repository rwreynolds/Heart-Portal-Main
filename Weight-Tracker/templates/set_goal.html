💡 Weight Goal Tips

🎯 Realistic Goals: Aim for 1-2 pounds per week for weight loss, or similar for weight gain.

📈 Consistency: Regular daily weigh-ins help track progress and identify trends.

🏥 Medical Guidance: Always consult your healthcare provider before setting weight goals, especially with heart conditions.

🔄 Flexibility: You can update your goal anytime as your needs change.

⚖️ Balance: Focus on overall health, not just the number on the scale.

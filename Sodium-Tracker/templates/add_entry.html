💡 Sodium Content Guidelines

  • Very Low: 35mg or less per serving
  • Low: 140mg or less per serving
  • Reduced: 25% less sodium than regular product
  • High: 600mg or more per serving
  • Daily Limit: Less than 2,300mg for most adults
  • Heart Healthy: Less than 1,500mg per day (ideal for heart failure)

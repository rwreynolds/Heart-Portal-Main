📋 Sodium Intake Guidelines

  • General Population: Less than 2,300mg per day
  • Heart Failure Patients: 1,500-2,000mg per day (consult your doctor)
  • Hypertension: Less than 1,500mg per day
  • Kidney Disease: Varies, follow medical advice
  • Athletes: May need higher intake during intense training

Note: Always consult with your healthcare provider before making significant changes to your sodium intake, especially if you have heart failure or other medical conditions.
